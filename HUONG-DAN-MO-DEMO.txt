SO THU CHI AI LOCAL - BAT DAU TAI DAY

1. Cai Python 3.12 64-bit (chon Add python.exe to PATH).
2. Giai nen toan bo ZIP, mo thu muc so-thu-chi-ai-local.
3. Bam setup-local.bat. Can Internet de cai thu vien lan dau.
4. Bam install-ocr-windows.bat va cai Tesseract o duong dan mac dinh.
5. Bam start-demo.bat, giu cua so terminal mo.
6. Mo http://localhost:8000, bam Trai nghiem voi du lieu mau.
